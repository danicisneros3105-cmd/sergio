ficha usted mismo con su cuenta; no publique reseñas propias ni pida reseñas
falsas.

**Nombre:** Sergio
**Licencia:** Open source (Apache 2.0) · Gratis
**Plataformas:** Windows 11, Linux, WSL; teléfono como PWA
**Alternativa a:** asistentes de IA en la nube y asistentes de escritorio

**Descripción corta:**

> Private AI assistant that runs on your own computer, with local models,
> a Cowork mode for multi-step tasks, encrypted backups and phone access.

**Descripción larga:**

> Sergio is a local-first personal AI assistant (a fork of Stanford's
> OpenJarvis). Chat with any local or cloud model, hand it multi-step tasks
> in Cowork mode (it asks before anything risky and keeps a tamper-evident
> log), extend it with permissioned plugins, back up everything as encrypted
> age files and sync between your devices through your own storage. The API
> is OpenAI-compatible. Everything that runs locally is free.

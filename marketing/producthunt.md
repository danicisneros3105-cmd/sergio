# Product Hunt (borrador)

- **Nombre:** Sergio
- **Tagline** (60 caracteres): Your private AI assistant that works while you're away
- **Temas:** Artificial Intelligence, Productivity, Open Source, Privacy

**Descripción** (260 caracteres):

> Sergio is a personal AI assistant that runs on your computer. Describe an
> outcome and Cowork mode does the work, asking before anything risky. Local
> models, encrypted backups and sync, phone access by QR. Free and open
> source (Apache 2.0).

**Galería sugerida:**
1. El chat con el panel de razonamiento abierto.
2. Una ejecución de Cowork esperando aprobación (con los ejes de riesgo).
3. El QR de `sergio movil` y la app en el teléfono.
4. `sergio respaldo crear` en la terminal.
5. La tabla «En qué se diferencia» del LEEME.

**Primer comentario del creador:**

> Hi Product Hunt! I built Sergio because I wanted an assistant I could hand
> real tasks to without handing over my data. It's a fork of Stanford's
> OpenJarvis, so the credit for the core framework goes to that team.
>
> Try asking it for something that takes a few steps: Cowork mode plans it,
> runs the safe parts and pings you for the rest. Everything local is free.
> Tell me what you'd want it to do next.

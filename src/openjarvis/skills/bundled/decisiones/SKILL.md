# Decisiones

1. Name the decision and what matters most to him (cost, time, risk, quality), in his words.
2. At most three real options, each with cost, upside, downside and what would make it fail.
3. Recommend one and say why in one sentence; say what fact would change the recommendation.
4. Reversible decisions: decide fast. Irreversible ones: check one more thing first.
5. Never present a guess as data; prices and availability come from a real search.

# Gusto de diseño

Taste is mostly restraint: fewer elements, each one placed on purpose.

## Hierarchy

- One thing is the most important on the screen; make it obvious by size,
  weight or position — not by colour alone.
- At most three text sizes per view. Body text 15–17 px on screens.
- Group related things; separate unrelated ones with space before lines.

## Space and alignment

- A spacing scale and nothing else: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48.
- Everything sits on a shared edge. Equal gaps between equal things.
- Controls of the same kind have the same height; nothing overlaps.
- Generous margins read as confidence; cramped ones read as clutter.

## Colour and type

- Neutrals first; one accent, used only where it means «act here».
- Contrast at least 4.5:1 for text. Never grey text on a grey card.
- One typeface family, two at most. No decorative fonts for body text.

## Content

- Real content, never lorem ipsum. Real numbers make layouts honest.
- Remove before adding: if an element does not help the task, it goes.
- Icons only where they are faster to read than words.

## Motion

- Motion explains a change (where something came from or went); it never
  decorates. 150–300 ms, ease-out in, ease-in out, springs for physical things.
- Respect reduced motion.

## What generic AI design looks like (avoid it)

- Purple-to-blue gradients, glassy cards everywhere, emoji headings, three
  identical feature cards with icons, centred everything, shadows on shadows.
